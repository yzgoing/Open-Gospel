# Code Of Conduct

This project is for technical collaboration.

## Expected Conduct

- Be direct and respectful.
- Challenge ideas with reasons.
- Keep claims falsifiable.
- Respect contributor time.
- Avoid personal attacks, harassment, and intimidation.

## Unacceptable Conduct

- Threats or abusive language
- Doxxing or privacy violations
- Deliberate sabotage of discussion or review
- Repeated bad-faith misrepresentation of other contributors

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or block contributors who repeatedly violate these rules.
